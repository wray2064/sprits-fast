# A cutout puppet by hand: two layers made parts, the arm hung on the torso
# where it is drawn, its joint placed in Draw part, the arm turned a quarter
# with the ring in Pose (Shift: it stops on 90, every pixel where a quarter
# turn sends it), and the pose captured as a frame of its own.
key ctrl+0
expect zoom 20
tool rectangle
colour 3060d0
drag 6 16 13 28
click-on Add
colour e08f40
drag 20 12 21 24
expect pixel 20 24 e08f40

click-on Puppet @1
wait 2
click-on Part from layer
click-on Layer 1
click-on Part from layer
wait 2

# The arm (Layer 2) hangs on the torso (Layer 1) where it is drawn.
click-on Layer 2
click-on Hangs from
wait 2
click-on Layer 1 @2
wait 2

# Its joint at the top of the arm: the middle of pixel (20, 12).
click-on Draw part
click-on Place joint
click 20 12
wait 2

# A quarter turn about (20.5, 12.5): 48 px out along x is script point
# 22.4 12, 48 px down is 20 14.4.
click-on Pose
hold shift
drag 22.4 12 20 14.4 24
let shift
wait 2
# (x, y) turned about (20.5, 12.5) is (32 - y, x - 8).
expect pixel 8 12 e08f40
expect pixel 20 13 e08f40
expect pixel 20 24 empty
expect pixel 6 16 3060d0

click-on Capture pose
wait 2
expect frames 2
expect frame 2
click-on Frames
wait 2
expect pixel 8 12 e08f40
expect pixel 20 24 empty
expect pixel 6 16 3060d0
