# A custom brush: Ctrl+B takes what is selected, and the pencil then draws a
# stroke whose tip is that shape, stamped at every pixel of the path.
tool pencil
colour e08f40
click 2 2
click 3 2
click 2 3
expect drawn 3 3

tool select
drag 2 2 3 3
key ctrl+b
key ctrl+d

# Five pixels across with an L of three: two rows, the top one a pixel
# longer -- eleven pixels, beside the three the brush was taken from. Its
# colour is the one already on the layer, so it carries on in that run.
tool pencil
drag 10 10 14 10
expect drawn 14 14
expect elements Pixels 1
