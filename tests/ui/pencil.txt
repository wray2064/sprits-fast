# The pencil, driven the way a hand drives it: a drag paints the whole way,
# undo and redo take it back and forth, the right button paints the second
# colour, and Shift+click draws a line from where the last stroke ended.
tool pencil
colour e08f40
drag 2 2 20 2
expect pixel 2 2 e08f40
expect pixel 11 2 e08f40
expect pixel 20 2 e08f40
expect drawn 19 19

key ctrl+z
expect drawn 0 0
key ctrl+y
expect drawn 19 19

drag 2 5 10 5 right
expect drawn 28 28
expect pixel 5 5 1a1a1f

hold shift
click 10 12
let shift
expect pixel 10 8 e08f40
expect drawn 35 35

# A diagonal drag at size 1 is pixel-perfect: one pixel per step, no L corners.
drag 14 14 24 24 6
expect drawn 46 46
