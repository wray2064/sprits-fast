# Ink modes, through the INK list in the Tool panel. Each keeps the pencil's
# stroke a path, clipped to what it may paint: lock alpha only where the
# layer draws, replace only where the other button's colour shows.
tool pencil
colour e08f40
drag 4 4 12 4
expect pixel 8 4 e08f40

# Lock alpha: a line across the first paints only where it crosses it.
click-on Ink mode
click-on Lock alpha
colour 3060c0
drag 8 1 8 8
expect pixel 8 4 3060c0
expect pixel 8 2 empty
expect pixel 8 6 empty
expect pixel 4 4 e08f40

# Replace: with the right button, the second colour goes only where the
# first shows -- the one blue pixel -- and nowhere else along the drag.
click-on Ink mode
click-on Replace colour
drag 2 4 14 4 right
expect pixel 8 4 1a1a1f
expect pixel 6 4 e08f40
expect pixel 13 4 empty
