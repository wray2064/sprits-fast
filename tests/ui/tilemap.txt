# A tilemap layer by hand: made from the Sprite menu with 8 x 8 tiles; a
# pixel drawn into an empty cell makes a tile; the tile placed in another
# cell; a pixel drawn into one placement shows in both; a flipped placing;
# the eraser empties a cell and undo brings it back; the bucket fills.
click-on Sprite
click-on New tilemap layer...
click-on 8 x 8
click-on Create
expect layers 2

tool pencil
colour e08f40
click 1 1
expect drawn 1 1

click-on Place tiles
click 9 1
expect pixel 9 1 e08f40
expect drawn 2 2

click-on Draw pixels
click 10 2
expect pixel 2 2 e08f40
expect drawn 4 4

click-on Place tiles
click-on Flip X
click 17 1
expect pixel 22 1 e08f40
expect pixel 21 2 e08f40

tool eraser
click 17 1
expect pixel 22 1 empty
key ctrl+z
expect pixel 22 1 e08f40

click-on Flip X
tool bucket
click 1 17
expect pixel 25 25 e08f40

# Frames: a duplicate keeps the cells as they are; an empty frame gets a
# tilemap layer of empty cells, the tiles still there to place.
key t
key ctrl+0
click-on + Frame
expect frames 2
expect pixel 9 1 e08f40
click-on + Empty
expect frames 3
expect layers 2
expect drawn 0 0
tool pencil
click 9 1
expect pixel 9 1 e08f40
