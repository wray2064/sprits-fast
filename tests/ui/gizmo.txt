# The transform gizmo by hand: a Rotate added in the Transform panel brings
# the Move tool and the gizmo; the ring dragged with Shift stops on 90
# exactly, and the line lands on exactly its pixels turned; Esc drops a drag;
# a typed angle is the angle; the arrow across stretches across only; the
# middle moves the sprite, and the gizmo goes with it.
#
# At this zoom (20) the pivot, the canvas's middle (16, 16), is script point
# 15.5 15.5 -- a script point p is canvas p + 0.5 -- and the ring is 64
# screen pixels, 3.2 canvas pixels, round it.
key ctrl+0
expect zoom 20
tool line
colour 3060d0
drag 12 17 26 3
expect pixel 12 17 3060d0
expect pixel 26 3 3060d0

click-on Rotate
expect tool move
expect rotation 0

# From 2.5 right and 1.5 down of the pivot (58 px out: on the ring) round to
# 1.5 left and 2.5 down -- a quarter turn, clockwise -- with Shift.
hold shift
drag 18 17 14 18 24
let shift
expect rotation 90
# (x, y) turned a quarter about the middle is (31 - y, x).
expect pixel 14 12 3060d0
expect pixel 28 26 3060d0
expect pixel 12 17 empty

# Esc in the middle of a drag: as it was, nothing to undo.
move 18 17
press
move 14 14
key escape
release
expect rotation 90

# Typed.
click-on ##angle
key ctrl+a
type 180
key enter
wait 2
expect rotation 180
expect pixel 19 14 3060d0
expect pixel 5 28 3060d0

# The arrow across, grabbed 30 px out and pulled to 60: twice as wide.
click-on Scale
expect scale 1 1
drag 17 15.5 18.5 15.5 24
expect scale 2 1
key ctrl+z
expect scale 1 1

# The middle: the sprite moved by whole pixels -- 3 across, 1 down.
drag 15.5 15.5 18.5 16.5 24
expect offset 3 1
# The line turned 180 had (19, 14); moved, (22, 15).
expect pixel 22 15 3060d0
expect pixel 19 14 empty

# Esc on a move: where it was.
move 18.5 16.5
press
move 12 12
key escape
release
expect offset 3 1

# The gizmo went with the sprite: its ring, round (19, 17) now, turns it a
# further quarter -- 270 -- about where it is. (x, y) turned 270 about the
# middle is (y, 31 - x); moved, (y + 3, 32 - x).
hold shift
drag 21 18 17 19 24
let shift
expect rotation 270
expect pixel 20 20 3060d0
expect pixel 6 6 3060d0
