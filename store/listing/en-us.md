## Description (up to 10,000 characters)

Sprit's'fast is a sprite and pixel-art editor built on a different idea: nothing you draw is ever turned into a fixed grid of pixels until the moment it is shown. A pencil stroke is the path the pencil took and the brush it took it with. A paint-bucket fill is the area it found. A rectangle is a rectangle. So when you turn a layer by 23 degrees, scale it, recolour a palette slot or change your mind ten steps back, the picture is drawn again cleanly from what you actually did, and nothing degrades.

It is free, works offline, and asks for no account. It collects nothing.

DRAW
- Pencil, spray, eraser, paint bucket, eyedropper, contour fill, rectangle, ellipse, line, curves and polygons, text, lasso and magic wand, with pen-tablet pressure.
- Pixel-perfect lines, symmetry across, down or both, tiled mode and tile grids, snap to grid, guides and slices.
- Shapes stay editable after you draw them. Dither patterns follow your palette. Rotate at any angle in pixel-art style, without the mush.

LAYERS AND PALETTES
- Layers with blend modes, opacity, groups and clipping; frames share what they should.
- Palettes in named slots, so a colour change repaints everywhere it was used. Ramps, presets, several palettes per file, swaps, and GPL, JASC, hex, ACT and PNG palette files in and out.

ANIMATE
- Frames, named cycles with loop and ping-pong modes, linked cels, onion skin, tweens, and playback at any speed.
- The timeline opens over the canvas when you want it and tucks away when you do not, or pins under it.
- Cutout puppets: parts, joints and sockets, posed by hand and captured to a frame.

BRING WORK IN AND OUT
- Opens its own .lsprite files, PNG, GIF, JPEG, BMP and Aseprite (.aseprite, .ase) files as editable work.
- Exports PNG at 1x to 16x (including indexed colour), animated GIF, APNG and WebP, and sprite sheets with a JSON description that game engines read.
- Reference images stay with the file, shown beside the work or over it.

MADE TO BE LIVED IN
- Nine themes, including true-black OLED, two high-contrast ones and a Famicom one; your own colour for every part of the interface; accent colours; interface size and density up to 150%.
- Panels dock, tab, float and resize; save your own layouts. A searchable user guide, command search and fully rebindable keys.
- English and Spanish. Automatic recovery copies, so a crash or a power cut does not cost you your work.

Sprit's'fast is open source (Apache-2.0) and built on the LiveSprite engine. Not affiliated with Aseprite or its makers; it reads their files so your work is not locked in.
